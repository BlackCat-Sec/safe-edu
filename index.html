Agenda

What this project is meant to do

SafeEd is not a generic content site. Its agenda is to make safety education actionable, context-aware, and easier to operationalize across schools, colleges, NGOs, communities, and workplaces.

Prevent earlier

Build pattern recognition around harassment, coercion, digital threats, and unsafe escalation.

Respond better

Help learners document incidents, locate official channels, and choose safe next steps under pressure.

Support recovery

Include emotional first-response principles, referral pathways, and non-judgmental language.

Scale responsibly

Keep the frontend maintainable, accessible, offline-capable, and ready for content ops or backend integration.
